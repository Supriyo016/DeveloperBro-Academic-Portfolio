Three things stand out.

**Pretraining buys a head start, not a finish.** After round 1, MobileNetV2 is at 58.3% and the Custom CNN at 53.9%. MobileNetV2 stays ahead until round 19. From round 20 onwards the Custom CNN leads, and it ends at 94.6% against 92.6%.

**The Custom CNN hadn't stopped.** Its last ten rounds gained about 3.8 points, from 90.8% to 94.6%. MobileNetV2 gained 2.2 over the same stretch. Thirty rounds was a budget decision, and the from-scratch model was the one still climbing when the budget ran out.

**EfficientNetB0 is in a different regime.** It climbs steadily, still gaining about 0.2 points per round over the last five rounds, but never reaches 80%.

It is worth reading these from the log rather than from the plot. The Custom CNN with FedAvg was at 77.9% at round 10 and first passed 90% at round 20. On a steep curve, "around round 10" and "round 20" are easy to confuse by eye.

## What I can't conclude from these curves

- **Each curve is one run.** I have no idea yet how much a curve would move with a different seed, so a gap of one or two points at a given round is not evidence of anything.
- **The curves are on the test set.** I log them for insight, and I report only the final round. If I picked the best round from these curves, the test set would stop being a fair test.
- **A longer budget could change the order.** The Custom CNN was still rising. FedProx and FedDyn were behind FedAvg but closing. With 50 or 100 rounds, the final ranking might differ.

Next I'll look at why EfficientNetB0 behaves so differently, because a pretrained model finishing 15 points behind a from-scratch one is the most surprising result so far.
